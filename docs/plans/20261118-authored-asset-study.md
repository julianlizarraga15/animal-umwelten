## Question and experiment

existing authored botanical geometry and material maps produce a visible step
forward under browser lighting. Use the isolated worktree at
`/tmp/animal-umwelten-graphics-study`. Preserve its initial screenshots.

Download a small CC0 botanical asset from Poly Haven using its public API.
Candidate IDs include `flower_gazania` and `potted_plant_01`. Verify actual
geometry, texture paths, and transfer sizes before selecting. Keep source URLs,
author information, license, and local files in an asset manifest.

Build an authored-asset comparison in the isolated study. Keep the procedural
version available for comparison. Load local glTF assets with their normal,
roughness, color, and alpha maps. Establish correct scale, normals, color space,
and transparent-leaf shadows before lighting changes. Use soft light and readable
shadow detail. Do not add garden decoration or hide flaws with blur.

The selected plant is a graphics specimen, not a substitute for Rudbeckia in the
scientific lesson. Label its actual source identity and keep it out of production.
