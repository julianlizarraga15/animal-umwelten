# Project status

Handoff updated: 2026-11-21 (enclosed afternoon patio)

## Current application

Umwelt Explorer opens inside a three-sided afternoon courtyard. Brick paving
meets the walls. Soil beds run along the sides and across the back, and
terracotta pots sit on the paving. Vines, border plants, and spikes grow from
that soil; the foreground framing leaves grow from pots. The loose canopy
leaves are gone.

The Rudbeckia, the bee, the pale web, the door, and the window stay readable.
The Spanish heading and dek still name the two lessons: flower contrast and
web vibration. Specimen captions and lesson controls stay hidden until
arrival. Renderings remain explanatory and do not claim literal animal
subjective experience. The upper plaster is still quieter than
`docs/art-direction/jardin-de-la-tarde.png`.

## Validation

check:browser` passed on Windows. The suite covered 1440×1000 and 390×844,
flower UV switching, reverse-petal inspection, web sectors, touch, reduced
motion, and WebGL recovery. It found no viewport occluders. The SwiftShader
patio frame reports 1,437,730 triangles, up from the prior 1,115,632.
Captures and metadata are in `test-results/patio/`. The build retains its
existing chunk-size warning. No physical device or deployment was tested.
The opening was also reviewed in the browser, including a look toward the
left wall.
